My Story

Most online stores don't struggle because of the product — they struggle because of a leaky checkout, broken tracking and ads running on guesswork.

That's where I start. Whether it's a Shopify store that looks fine but loses customers on the way to checkout, a GA4 setup that counts the wrong events, a Google Ads budget quietly leaking into the wrong searches, or YouTube videos nobody can find — the fix usually begins with an honest audit rather than a bigger budget.

I work across Shopify store setup and optimization, GA4 and GTM tracking, Google Ads, TikTok Ads and YouTube video SEO. Some clients need one of those; most need two or three working together. I'd rather tell you which one matters right now than sell you a bundle you don't need.

Tracking comes first. Before any ad budget is spent, I make sure GA4 and Google Tag Manager are recording the events that matter — purchases, add-to-carts and checkouts — so every decision after that is based on what actually happened.

If you work with me, you get direct communication — no account managers in between — clear reporting, and someone who treats your budget the way I'd treat my own.
