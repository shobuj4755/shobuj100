About Me

Sales Growth That Starts With Clean Data

I'm a Shopify and e-commerce specialist who works with online store owners to set up, optimize and grow their stores. I care less about how a campaign looks in a report and more about whether the store converts, the tracking is accurate and the ad spend pays back.

My approach is simple: build the store properly, track every important event with GA4 and Google Tag Manager, then run Google Ads and TikTok Ads on top of data you can trust — with YouTube video SEO bringing in search traffic that keeps working long after you publish.

  • Data-driven decisions, not guesswork
  • Tracking verified before ad budget is spent

  • Direct communication, no account managers
